Accountant

Accounting resumes are screened on three facts before anything else is read: what you are qualified as, which systems you have worked in, and how big a close you have owned.

6 min readUpdated 11 September 2026Written by the PlainSheet team

What gets looked at first

Before anyone reads a sentence, they check whether you clear the bar. For this role that means:

  • Qualification and status: ACA, ACCA, CIMA, StB, part-qualified and how far.
  • Company size and turnover, because a €4m close and a €400m close are different jobs.
  • Which ERP. SAP, Netsuite, Datev, Xero, Sage — this is a filter, not a nice-to-have.
  • Whether you have owned a month-end or supported one.
  • Audit, tax, consolidation, group reporting: which of these you have actually done.

Everything on the page below is there to answer one of those, or it is not there.

Why those lines and not the usual ones

Every weak version below is a real sentence that appears on thousands of resumes. Nothing was invented to fix them — the same facts are simply stated.

Says little

Responsible for month-end close and financial reporting.

Says the same thing, usefully

Own the month-end close for three legal entities, €120m combined turnover: accruals, intercompany, stock provisioning and the reporting pack.

Says little

Improved efficiency of the finance function.

Says the same thing, usefully

Took the close from day nine to day five by moving intercompany reconciliation into the previous week and automating two manual journals.

The pattern is the one from the bullet points guide: what you did, how you did it, and what changed as a result.

Common questions

I am part-qualified. How do I write that?

Exactly as it is: "ACCA, 11 of 13 papers, finalist September 2026". Vagueness here is read as concealment, and the number of papers is the first thing asked at interview anyway.

Does the accounting software really matter that much?

Yes. Recruiters filter on it because the learning curve is real. Name the version and the modules, not just the vendor.
