Administrative assistant

Administrative work is judged on what stopped going wrong, which is the hardest kind of achievement to write down and the reason most of these resumes read thin.

6 min readUpdated 11 September 2026Written by the PlainSheet team

What gets looked at first

Before anyone reads a sentence, they check whether you clear the bar. For this role that means:

  • Which systems and to what depth. Outlook and Excel are assumed; the ERP, the CRM and the finance system are not.
  • Who you supported, and how many of them.
  • Whether you have held a budget, however small.
  • Diary and travel complexity: how many calendars, how many countries.
  • Discretion, shown by what you were trusted with rather than claimed.

Everything on the page below is there to answer one of those, or it is not there.

Why those lines and not the usual ones

Every weak version below is a real sentence that appears on thousands of resumes. Nothing was invented to fix them — the same facts are simply stated.

Says little

Provided administrative support to senior management and the wider team.

Says the same thing, usefully

Support three directors — diaries, international travel, board packs, and the expenses nobody submits until asked twice.

Says little

Responsible for ordering supplies and managing suppliers.

Says the same thing, usefully

Renegotiated the cleaning, coffee and print contracts at renewal, saving SEK 140,000 a year with no change in service.

The pattern is the one from the bullet points guide: what you did, how you did it, and what changed as a result.

Common questions

My work is invisible when it goes well. How do I show it?

Write the consequence. "New starters now have accounts and equipment on day one, which was not true before" describes work nobody notices, in a way a reader immediately understands.

Should I list Microsoft Office?

Only with detail behind it. "Excel to pivot tables and Power Query" is a claim; "Microsoft Office" on its own suggests it was worth mentioning, which is not the impression you want.
