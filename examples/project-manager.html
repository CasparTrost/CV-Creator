Project manager

Project management resumes fail in a specific way: they describe the methodology and leave out the project. Budgets, headcount and outcomes are the content.

7 min readUpdated 11 September 2026Written by the PlainSheet team

What gets looked at first

Before anyone reads a sentence, they check whether you clear the bar. For this role that means:

  • Budget and team size. Running a €80,000 project is a different job from running a €8 million one.
  • Sector, because domain knowledge transfers and process knowledge mostly does not.
  • Whether you delivered, and what it cost when you did not.
  • Certifications: PRINCE2, PMP, IPMA, Scrum, with years.
  • How senior the people were that you had to persuade.

Everything on the page below is there to answer one of those, or it is not there.

Why those lines and not the usual ones

Every weak version below is a real sentence that appears on thousands of resumes. Nothing was invented to fix them — the same facts are simply stated.

Says little

Responsible for managing large, complex projects using agile and waterfall methodologies.

Says the same thing, usefully

ERP replacement across four plants: NOK 38m, eighteen months, a core team of fourteen plus three external suppliers. Delivered a month late and within budget.

Says little

Managed stakeholder relationships at all levels of the organisation.

Says the same thing, usefully

Ran the steering committee for the CFO and two plant directors — monthly decision papers, and the awkward conversations when a date moved.

The pattern is the one from the bullet points guide: what you did, how you did it, and what changed as a result.

Common questions

Should I list every project I have run?

No. Three or four in detail, then one line summarising the rest: "Eleven further projects, NOK 1m to 5m, all delivered." Detail on the recent and the relevant.

What if a project failed?

A project that was stopped for a good reason is fine to include if you say what you did about it. "Recommended cancellation after the pilot, saving the remaining €400k of budget" is a stronger line than most successes.
