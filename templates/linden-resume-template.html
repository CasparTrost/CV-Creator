Single column, colour band

One column, top to bottom, with a coloured header across the full width. Applicant tracking systems read single-column documents in the order you wrote them, which is not true of sidebars.

Pick this one if

  • You are uploading through an online application portal
  • You want the safest structure for automated parsing
  • Your content is long enough to fill the width
