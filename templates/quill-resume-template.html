Quill

Quiet header on white

No colour field at all. Your name sits in the accent colour on white, with a rule beneath it. The tinted sidebar is the only fill on the page.

Pick this one if

  • You find coloured banners too loud
  • You work in law, finance or public administration
  • You want colour used sparingly rather than as a background
Use Quill
  • StructureTwo columns
  • PhotoYes
  • Header treatmentNone
  • PagesTwo, second one optional
  • ExportPDF and Word, free
